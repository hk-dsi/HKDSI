About HKDSI

The Hong Kong Diaspora Studies Initiative (HKDSI) was first formed in 2024 by a group of social scientists who share a common interest in the scholarship about Hong Kong diaspora. Our goal is to document and research about the in-betweenness of Hongkongers, as they depart from their homeland and settle in a new host country. Our research goals are to:

  1. Understand how transnational linkages continue to shape Hong Kong diaspora.

關於我們

香港離散研究計劃（HKDSI）於2024年由一群對香港離散群體研究抱有共同興趣的社會科學學者所成立。 我們的目標是記錄並研究香港人在離開家園、遷移至新居住國的過程中所經歷的「夾縫」與「過渡」狀態。 我們的研究目標包括：

  1. 了解跨國聯繫如何持續影響香港離散群體的發展。

The findings from our survey and data analysis will be shared here as we progress. 我們的調查結果與數據分析將會隨著研究進展在此分享。
